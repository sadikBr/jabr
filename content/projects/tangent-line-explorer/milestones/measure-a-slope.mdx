Everything in this project rests on one small tool: a function that is handed
a curve and a point, and returns how steep the curve is there.

You know the idea already. Zoom in on a smooth curve and it looks straight;
the slope of that straight piece is the derivative.

<Interactive id="tangent-zoom" props={{ fn: "x^3 - x", x0: 1 }} />

A computer cannot take a limit, but it can use a very small interval. The
obvious choice is the secant from $x$ to $x + h$. There is a better one: step
the same distance to **both sides** and take the slope across the pair:

$$
f'(x) \approx \frac{f(x + h) - f(x - h)}{2h}
$$

The errors from the two sides are equal and opposite, and mostly cancel. With
the same $h$, this version is thousands of times more accurate. For a
parabola it is exactly right, whatever $h$ is.

### Your task

Complete `slope`. Notice that its first argument is itself a function: in
Python a function can be passed around like any other value.
